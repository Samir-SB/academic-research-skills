# Research Paper Summary

## Energy Centered and QoS Aware Services Selection for Internet of Things

---

## Key Findings

### Problem Addressed
- **IoT Service Selection**: Choosing optimal services from registries
- **Energy Efficiency**: Battery-powered devices require energy-aware selection
- **QoS Awareness**: Response time, reliability, cost attributes
- **Challenge**: Balance energy consumption with service quality

### Proposed Approach
- **Energy-Centric Optimization**: Prioritizes energy efficiency
- **Multi-Objective Framework**: Balances energy and QoS
- **Pareto Optimization**: Finds trade-off solutions
- **Energy-Aware Fitness**: Combines energy cost with QoS

### Technical Contributions
1. Energy-aware selection model
2. Multi-objective fitness function
3. Pareto front analysis
4. Energy consumption estimation

### Evaluation Results
- Significant energy savings vs QoS-only
- Acceptable QoS trade-offs
- Pareto-optimal solutions
- Improved makespan
