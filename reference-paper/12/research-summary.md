# Research Paper Summary

## Composing Energy Services in a Crowdsourced IoT Environment

---

## Key Findings

### Problem Addressed
- **Crowdsourced IoT**: Energy services from distributed users/devices
- **Service Composition**: Combining multiple energy services
- **Challenge**: Heterogeneous providers, dynamic availability
- **Significance**: Enables peer-to-peer energy sharing

### Proposed Approach
- **Crowdsourcing Model**: Users as energy service providers
- **Dynamic Composition**: Adaptive service combination
- **QoS Management**: Energy-specific quality metrics
- **Incentive Mechanism**: Rewards for participation

### Technical Contributions
1. Crowdsourced service model
2. Dynamic composition algorithm
3. Energy QoS evaluation
4. Incentive framework

### Evaluation Results
- Improved service availability
- Better user engagement
- Energy balance achieved
- Acceptable composition time
