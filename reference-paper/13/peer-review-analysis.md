This paper proposes a novel concept of crowdsourcing energy as a service (CEaaS), where distributed users and devices can contribute excess energy to create a shared energy pool. The approach enables dynamic energy sharing where prosumers offer energy services to consumers, forming an energy-as-a-service marketplace.
