# Research Paper Summary

## Crowdsourcing Energy as a Service

---

## Key Findings

### Problem Addressed
- **Crowdsourcing Energy**: Users as energy service providers
- **Energy-as-a-Service**: Marketplace for energy sharing
- **Challenge**: Coordinating distributed energy resources
- **Significance**: Enables peer-to-peer energy trading

### Proposed Approach
- **CEaaS Framework**: Crowdsourcing energy as service model
- **Service Marketplace**: Platform for energy trading
- **Dynamic Allocation**: Real-time energy distribution
- **Quality Management**: Energy QoS metrics

### Technical Contributions
1. CEaaS architecture design
2. Market mechanism for energy trading
3. Service discovery for energy providers
4. Quality assurance via SLAs

### Evaluation Results
- Marketplace feasibility demonstrated
- Practical trading performance
- User participation potential
- Energy efficiency achieved
